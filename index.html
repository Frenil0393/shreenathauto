06 / A little clarity

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Before your next step, here are a few things worth knowing.

What makes Shreenath a trusted RTO adviser?

Our team brings decades of experience, local knowledge, and a personal approach to RTO work. We explain the process and costs upfront, help organise your paperwork, and keep you informed as your application progresses.

Are all services available in Dhoraji?

Yes. Our Dhoraji office on Jetpur Road provides our full range of RTO consultancy services, including registration, ownership transfers, driving licence assistance, and renewals. Speak with Divyesh or Jasmin to plan your visit.

How do I know which documents to bring?

Call us with the service you need and a few details about your vehicle. We’ll explain the documents relevant to your application before you visit. Requirements can differ by service and individual circumstances.

How long will my RTO work take?

Timelines depend on the service, document readiness, appointment availability, and the relevant authority. After reviewing your requirements, we’ll explain the expected next steps and keep you updated. Final processing and approvals remain with the authority.

Can you explain the fees before I begin?

Yes. We explain the applicable government charges and our service fees before you proceed. If the scope of your work changes, we’ll discuss it with you so you can make an informed decision.
