Information you choose to share

The enquiry form asks for your name, telephone number, chosen service, preferred branch, and message. An email address is optional. The form assembles an email draft in your browser; preparing or copying it does not submit your information to a website form server. It reaches us only when you send the email or contact us directly.

We use information you send to respond, understand your requirements, arrange the requested assistance, and keep records needed for that work. Please avoid identity documents, bank details, or other sensitive information in an initial enquiry. Our team can explain any later document requirements separately.

Appearance preferences

If you change the appearance, this browser stores your light, dark or system mode and chosen accent colour. These preferences stay on your device and are not sent to our Analytics integration. They remain until you reset appearance or clear site data. System mode follows your device’s colour-scheme setting.

Cookie and analytics choices

A first-party local-storage preference records your choice and its expiry for up to 180 days. Optional Google Analytics loads only when a valid measurement ID is configured and you accept analytics. You can reject optional analytics without losing access to the website, or change your choice through Cookie settings.

When enabled with consent, Analytics receives information such as pages visited, browser/device information, and general interactions such as selecting a phone link or preparing an enquiry. Our custom events do not contain names, phone numbers, email addresses, or enquiry text. Query strings and fragments are omitted from the page locations supplied by our integration. Advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled by the website configuration.

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals keep this integration off. Withdrawing consent stops further measurement by this integration and attempts to clear accessible first-party Google Analytics cookies. It does not erase information already received by Google. See the cookie policy for storage details.

External providers and hosting

Our hosting provider, InfinityFree, uses a browser security check that requires JavaScript and cookies. This operational check is separate from optional Google Analytics and is controlled by the host. InfinityFree may process ordinary server logs such as IP addresses, timestamps, requested pages, and browser information to deliver and secure the website. The website requests fonts from Google Fonts and visual libraries from jsDelivr, which receive connection information when those resources load. These resource requests are separate from optional analytics and are not controlled by the analytics checkbox.

Map links open Google Maps only when selected. Your email provider handles messages you choose to send. These providers may process information on infrastructure outside India under their own policies. See Google’s privacy policy and jsDelivr’s privacy policy.

Sharing and retention

Enquiry information is used by the team handling your request. Where you engage us for a service, we discuss the information needed for the relevant authority or service provider. Information may also need to be retained or disclosed to meet applicable legal obligations. We do not use enquiry contents for advertising through this website.

Enquiry and service records are kept for as long as needed for the purpose for which they were provided and any applicable record-keeping obligations. The website’s cookie preference expires after 180 days. Retention of information already sent to an external provider is governed by that provider and the configured account settings.

Installation and offline storage

The installable website stores a small offline contact page, its styles and icons in your browser’s Cache Storage. Normal pages are requested from the network so online visits receive current content. We do not store enquiry contents, full website pages or analytics responses in this offline cache. Old versions of this cache are removed when the website’s service worker updates. You can remove this data by clearing the website’s site data in your browser; uninstalling an app alone may not clear browser storage. This functional storage is independent of optional analytics.

Language preference

Choose English or Gujarati on your first visit, or use the language button beside the appearance control at any time. We store your choice on this device under shreenath_language_v1 until you change it or clear site data. If local storage is unavailable, we try session storage; if both are blocked, the choice lasts only for this page. This functional preference is independent of optional analytics.

Your requests and concerns

You may contact shreenath.auto3930@gmail.com or +91 98258 66262 to ask about information you have shared, request correction or deletion where applicable, withdraw permission for optional processing, or raise a concern. Please describe your request without including sensitive documents. We may need enough information to identify the relevant enquiry and verify the request before taking action.

Updates

We may update this page when website features or our practices change. The date at the top identifies this version. A change in the analytics configuration requires a fresh choice through the consent controls.
