Ways to use the site

The pages include headings, labelled form fields, a skip-to-content link, visible keyboard focus, and navigation using ordinary links. FAQ questions can be opened with a keyboard. Cookie preferences use a dialog with a close control and Escape-key support.

Motion preferences

The website responds to your operating system’s reduced-motion preference by removing non-essential movement and smooth-scroll effects. Live scenes have a Pause motion control when animation is available. Browsers without the required graphics support receive lightweight still illustrations. Scrolling and page content remain available.

Appearance and colour

Use the Change appearance button in the header to choose light, dark or system mode and an accent colour. System mode follows your device. Custom accents are adjusted for readable text. Your choice is remembered when browser storage is available; Reset restores the original colours and system mode.

English and Gujarati

The language button beside the appearance control reopens the language chooser. Your selection is remembered when browser storage is available. Gujarati uses dedicated Gujarati fonts and spacing. Language transitions follow your reduced-motion preference. Without JavaScript, the original English pages remain available.

Smaller screens and text

Layouts adapt to smaller viewports. Forms use readable input text, and page content is intended to remain accessible when enlarged. Tables with several columns can be scrolled horizontally. You can use your browser’s zoom and text controls.

On mobile, open Menu to find Appearance and Language. General page text and imagery have selection and right-click deterrents; form fields, contact details and policy text remain available to select and copy. Browser zoom and keyboard shortcuts remain available.

Alternative contact

If something prevents you from using the website, call +91 98258 66262 or email shreenath.auto3930@gmail.com. Tell us which page or task caused difficulty and how you would prefer us to help. You can also visit either branch.

Ongoing improvement

We welcome reports of barriers and aim to improve the site as issues are identified. The features described here are design intentions and implemented controls, not a claim of independent accessibility certification.
