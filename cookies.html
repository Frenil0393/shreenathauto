Cookies and local storage

Cookies are small records a browser can send with requests to a website. Local storage is a separate browser store that is not automatically sent with each request. We use local storage to remember your privacy choice and, when you change them, your appearance preferences. Optional Analytics may set cookies only when configured and accepted.

Accepting, rejecting and changing your mind

The first-visit notice offers rejection, acceptance when analytics is available, and detailed preferences. Closing the preferences dialog without saving does not grant consent. We ask again after a saved choice expires or when the Analytics measurement configuration changes.

Select Cookie settings in any footer to change your choice. When analytics is switched off, our integration disables further measurement and attempts to remove accessible first-party Analytics cookies for this domain. This does not delete data previously sent to Google. You can also clear site data through your browser; doing so removes the saved preference and the notice will appear again.

Installation and offline storage

The installable website stores a small offline contact page, its styles and icons in your browser’s Cache Storage. Normal pages are requested from the network so online visits receive current content. We do not store enquiry contents, full website pages or analytics responses in this offline cache. Old versions of this cache are removed when the website’s service worker updates. You can remove this data by clearing the website’s site data in your browser; uninstalling an app alone may not clear browser storage. This functional storage is independent of optional analytics.

Language preference

Choose English or Gujarati on your first visit, or use the language button beside the appearance control at any time. We store your choice on this device under shreenath_language_v1 until you change it or clear site data. If local storage is unavailable, we try session storage; if both are blocked, the choice lasts only for this page. This functional preference is independent of optional analytics.

Browser controls

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals keep this Analytics integration off, even if an earlier preference allowed it. If browser storage is blocked, your choice applies in memory for the current page and may need to be made again after navigating or reloading.

Analytics may also be blocked by extensions, network settings, or browser features. The website remains usable when optional analytics is unavailable.

External resources

Google Fonts and jsDelivr deliver fonts and visual libraries. Requests to them are separate from Analytics and can disclose connection information such as an IP address. The analytics preference does not block these resources. Google Maps and your email provider open only when you follow the respective links. This website is hosted on InfinityFree. Its browser security system requires JavaScript and cookies and may set an operational verification cookie independently of our analytics choice. Hosting cookies and server logs are controlled by the hosting provider.
